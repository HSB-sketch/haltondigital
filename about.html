Our Founder

Meet Harry

👨‍💼

Harry has been the backbone of Milton's tech community for over 20 years. A resident since 2006, he watched local small businesses struggle with technology they didn't understand and services they couldn't afford.

With an IT degree, deep expertise in networking and datacenter infrastructure, and years of experience as a hardware specialist, Harry decided to do something about it.

The Mission

To provide small businesses in the Halton region with enterprise-grade technology solutions at competitive prices. No call centers. No runaround. Just honest, expert tech support from someone who genuinely cares about your success.

"Our job isn't to sell you technology. It's to solve your problems and make your business run better."

— Harry

Journey

How We Grew

2004

The Beginning

Harry starts freelance tech work, helping local businesses with networking, hardware, and custom builds. Word of mouth grows the client base quickly.

2010

Specialization

Realizing small businesses need complete tech solutions, Harry expands into web design and IPTV services to be a one-stop shop.

2016

Canadian Infrastructure Focus

After seeing too many businesses use overseas hosting, Harry commits to Canadian providers and datacentres. Your data stays home.

2020

Building a Team

With 500+ projects and hundreds of happy clients, Harry brings on expert developers, specialists, and a sales manager to scale responsibly.

2024

Halton Digital & AI

Officially founded to continue serving underserved small businesses with cutting-edge solutions at fair prices. More than just a service provider — a technology partner.
